# ADR-2147 — Inferences carry a re-checkable derivation certificate and proposals carry a consequence delta

## Context
The corpus's inferred closure (`ontology-inferred.ttl`, 81,396 subsumptions on generation `1ec5774ad`) and the `WHELK_INCONSISTENT` blocker (ADR-2125) both rest on one reasoner's word. ADR-2128 §3 asks the build to report the classes a new `rdfs:domain` newly entails before the change is accepted; nothing computes that. `PatchProposal` carries a text diff; one `rdfs:domain` line is one line of diff. Open Ontologies (fabio-rovai/open-ontologies v2.0.1, MIT) writes derivation certificates that a Lean 4 checker with a soundness theorem re-verifies, and ships a conservativity plan. Spike on the published corpus (2026-10-20, `scratchpad/SPIKE-open-ontologies-2026-10-20.md`): its RDFS certificate reproduces Whelk's named closure exactly (81,396 of 81,396); its checker accepts the 731k-line certificate in 9.5 s and refuses a forged line in 8.3 s; its `plan` conservativity ran over 16 min at 12.5 GB and its shape diff degrades to a full reload on our 23k restriction blank nodes. Its binary is glibc-linked and pulls axum, rmcp, arrow and oxigraph 0.5. The same spike showed `ontology-class.shacl.ttl` failing every class under a W3C evaluator: labels are `@en`, the shape said `xsd:string`, and the in-house evaluator hid it.

## Decision
1. **The engine side is internalised, the checker is not.** The engine is the `rdfs-certificate` crate (crates.io 0.1.0, MIT OR Apache-2.0, maintained in the ontomark workspace under ontomark ADR-OM-009 and consumed by both producers; amended 2026-10-20, it first landed as `crates/vault/src/certificate.rs` in this repository): an RDFS forward chainer over N-Triples-spelled terms that records every step (rdfs2, 3, 5, 7, 9, 11, premises in the checker's order) and writes the `oo-cert/1` directory: `asserted.tsv`, `derivations.tsv`, `asserted.sha256`, `scope.tsv`. `crates/vault/src/certificate.rs` is the adapter that spells the vault's `Graph` into it. The format and rule table are adapted from Open Ontologies under MIT with the notice kept verbatim inside the crate (`LICENSE-UPSTREAM.txt`; this repository's `NOTICE` points there). The Lean checker `oo-cert` is never vendored and never a build dependency: its independence from the engine is the warrant. CI and reviewers run it as a pinned release binary; `tests/certificate_oo_cert.rs` runs it when `OO_CERT_BIN` is set and says so when it is not. No sidecar service, no daemon, no MCP server.
2. **`vault certify --out <dir>`** certifies the graph `vault build` publishes and reports `whelk_parity`: the certificate's named `rdfs:subClassOf` closure must equal `whelk::reason(...).inferred`. The command exits 1 when it does not. Parity is structural today — the emitter puts restrictions only on the superclass side, so every named entailment is subsumption transitivity — and the review trigger names the change that would end it.
3. **`vault propose` carries `consequences`.** Both the base and the patched corpus are certified inside `Assessment::of`, and `PatchProposal.consequences` (`ConsequenceDelta`) records the verdict word (`conservative_under_rule_table` / `not_conservative_under_rule_table`), conclusions added and lost over the **closure** (asserted and derived; derived-only misreports a consequence that becomes asserted), counts by predicate, bounded examples, the number of blank-node-bearing triples not compared, and the proposed certificate's `asserted.sha256`. `--certificate-dir` writes `base/`, `proposed/` and `consequences.json` beside the proposal. The delta is shown to the signer and is never a blocker: Whelk inconsistency remains the only reasoning blocker (Invariant 9). The 31402 content is the proposal JSON, so the field reaches the forum; the certificate files do not (305 MB), the digest does.
4. **SHACL shape and evaluator are W3C-correct.** `ontology-class.shacl.ttl` says `sh:nodeKind sh:Literal` on `rdfs:label`; the in-house evaluator types a `@language` value as `rdf:langString` (not `xsd:string`) and enforces `sh:Literal` and `sh:BlankNode`.
5. **Not adopted:** Open Ontologies' `plan`/`apply`/`rollback` lifecycle, its store, its SHACL and tableaux engines, its MCP server. ADR-2116 stands: the only write path is `vault propose` into the forum.

## Consequences
- Every inferred subsumption the Loom serves can be re-derived by a party who trusts neither Whelk nor this crate, from two files and a pinned binary. An auditor's cost is ~9 s per generation.
- The signer of a 31403 sees the semantic size of a change. On the published corpus one `rdfs:domain` on `vc:requires` is 206,476 new consequences (5,036 classes retyped as instances of the DAO class and its ancestors); reparenting `technical-standard` is 1,356 named subsumptions over 237 classes; a leaf's new parent is a handful.
- `vault propose` costs two more fixpoints: ~1.2 s each on the full corpus, and ~300 MB per written certificate when `--certificate-dir` is given. Without the flag nothing is written.
- The rule table is RDFS, so the certificate warrants exactly the named closure Whelk computes here and not EL reasoning in general; `whelk_parity` is the check that this stays true, and `defines-as` definitions (ADR-2124) already classify through Whelk only.
- Blank-node-bearing consequences are counted, not compared, because labels are minted per build. A proposal that only changes a restriction's filler reads as conservative over the named closure; the count says how much was not compared.
- Enabled by: the `ontology-publish.yml` build job runs `vault certify` and re-checks the certificate with the pinned `oo-cert` v2.0.1 (by sha256) on every generation it builds, uploading the reports and the certificate as artefacts; the proposal list in the client (`OntologyProposalList.tsx`) renders the `consequences` block as a chip and a details panel beside the gate chips; the agentbox `vaultSrc` pin moves to the merge commit so the baked `vault` carries `certify` and the delta (agentbox ADR-2108 pin log). The publish pipeline of the corpus repository itself builds with the released `ontomark`, which owns build and gate; `propose` stays in `vault` (agentbox ADR-2136), so the delta needs no ontomark change.
